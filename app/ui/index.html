💡 Understanding SigLIP 2 Similarity Scores

Algorithm: Cosine Similarity between two 768-dimensional L2-normalized float32 vectors: similarity = Vector_A • Vector_B.

🟢 Strong Match (+0.08 to +0.20+)

High likelihood to rank at the top. The image content strongly reflects the prompt concept.

🟡 Moderate / Context (+0.03 to +0.08)

Weak or partial match (e.g., outdoor landscape vs. specific hiking activity). Good for broad recall.

🔴 Distractor / Negative (< 0.00)

Semantic contradiction or unrelated concepts.
